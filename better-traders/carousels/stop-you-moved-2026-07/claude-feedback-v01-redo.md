# Claude Worker feedback for The Stop You Moved v01 redo

Source contact sheet: `v01-review-pack/tbt-stop-you-moved-contact-sheet-v01-review.png`

Aaron note: story arc is good, but Chat Image 2 visuals fall flat: no variety in images, poses, expression, perspective, or imagery storytelling. Focus on characters, not AI graphics/holographic screens/external displays.

## Creative Director Critique

The story works. The pictures don't. All seven frames are effectively the same photograph: Dani in the same olive top, same three-quarter seated angle, same distance, same flat expression, same wall of blue holographic charts glowing behind her. The text carries 100% of the narrative while the imagery carries 0%.

Root problems:
1. One shot, repeated: no change in scale, angle, or lens height.
2. Expression is frozen: the emotional arc needs clarity → temptation → rationalization → honest reckoning → resolve → calm.
3. The screens are doing the acting: glowing charts pull focus off the human.
4. No props, no hands, no physical story: the rule she writes down needs a tangible journal/chart prop.

Fix: kill the screen wall, promote Dani. Tell the emotional story through shot scale, face, posture, and one physical prop: a paper trading journal.

## Slide-by-slide redo plan

1. Wide establishing: Dani composed at desk, looking down at printed chart/journal.
2. Insert/macro: hand + pen laying a clean horizontal line across a paper chart.
3. Medium close / slight over-shoulder: Dani leans toward chart; brow tightening, lips pressed.
4. Tight close-up: rationalizing half-second; eyes flick away; half face in shadow.
5. Direct close-up: honest question moment; she looks at the viewer.
6. High-angle insert: open journal; pen writing the rule.
7. Medium-wide resolution: Dani sits back with closed journal, relaxed and calm.

Rules: shot-scale ladder; thread the paper journal through; expression is the throughline; screens dim/off/background only; same face, olive wardrobe, desk, and color grade.
