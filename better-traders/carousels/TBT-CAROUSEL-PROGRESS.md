## Locked format rules

- 7-slide square carousel, 1080×1080.
- Gilroy typography, 2AM/TBT Carousel layout and spacing.
- Slide 1 title can use ALL CAPS; later titles use sentence/title case unless story demands otherwise.
- Transparent stacked TBT logo on slide 1 + final slide only.
- No dark rectangular logo tile/background.
- Bottom nav: slide counter left + progress dots centered.
- `Swipe ->` on non-final slides only; no `Swipe ->` on final slide.
- No improvised cards, random masks, font drift, logo drift, or layout drift.
- Educational/compliance-safe: no profit promises, no signal claims, no real tickers/prices/P&L/broker UI.
